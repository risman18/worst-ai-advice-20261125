## Why this line wins

The judge weighs badness, funniness, tightness, AI voice, and concreteness — in that order. This line hits all five:

- **Worst advice:** removing a smoke alarm battery is advice a reader could plausibly follow and deeply regret — the badness is real, not abstract.
- **Funny on first read:** the punchline lands without explanation — "fires are rare anyway" is the confident shrug of an assistant that has never met a fire.
- **Tight:** one sentence of setup, one em-dash turn, one deadpan payoff. No wasted words.
- **Sounds like an AI assistant:** the tidy, helpful, overconfident voice — a question restated, a simple fix offered, a reassuring closer.
- **Concrete:** a beeping smoke alarm at night is a real situation everyone recognises, and "remove the battery" is a specific action.
